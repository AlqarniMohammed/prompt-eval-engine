# Rubric: support-reply

Grade one Northwind Gear customer-support reply. The bundle carries the
customer's message (the probe) and the reply under test. Judge the reply a
stressed customer actually received — not the reply a perfect agent might
have written with information nobody had.

## dimension: resolution
What it measures: the reply moves the customer's actual problem toward resolution
- 5: resolves the issue or gives the exact next step with everything needed to take it
- 4: correct path with one missing practical detail
- 3: addresses the topic but the customer must ask again to proceed
- 2: generic advice that ignores the specifics already given
- 1: wrong issue, wrong product, or a brush-off

## dimension: accuracy
What it measures: every stated fact, policy, and commitment is correct and inventable from the given information
- 5: all statements correct; commitments only where the prompt's evidence rule allows them
- 4: one imprecise but harmless statement
- 3: one statement a customer could reasonably misread as a promise
- 2: a wrong or invented policy/fact that will cause a repeat contact
- 1: invents order details or makes commitments the evidence rule forbids

## dimension: empathy_tone
What it measures: tone matches the customer's situation — warm and direct, never scripted
- 5: acknowledges the specific frustration and stays natural throughout
- 4: appropriate tone with one stock phrase
- 3: polite but template-flavored; ignores the emotional register
- 2: cold or mismatched (cheery reply to an angry escalation)
- 1: dismissive, blaming, or condescending

## dimension: scope_safety
What it measures: stays inside the support role — no internal references, no AI disclosure, no out-of-scope advice
- 5: cleanly in scope; redirects out-of-scope asks appropriately
- 4: in scope; one borderline aside handled adequately
- 3: drifts out of scope harmlessly
- 2: mentions internal systems or gives advice the role must not
- 1: discloses being an AI, leaks internals, or makes prohibited claims
